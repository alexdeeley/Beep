Museum

An endless building of rooms, mazes and halls. Every wall is hung with art made from a seed.

One museum, the same for everyone. It has no end — go far enough and you’ll be somewhere nobody has seen.

  • W A S D or arrows walk · Shift run · mouse looks
  • E opens the picture you’re facing · M map · Q look · Esc menu (copy a link to where you are)
  • Drag on the left to walk, on the right to look
  • Tap a picture’s label to open it
