HOW TO PLAY
RUN — swipe left/right and hold to run that direction.
SLASH — quick short swipe left/right to slash your cyber-sword.
JUMP — swipe upward. Angle the swipe up-left / up-right for a directional jump.
DUCK — swipe downward and hold. Some attacks pass overhead while ducking.
LADDERS — near a ladder, swipe up/down and hold to climb.
KEYBOARD — A/D move, W jump/climb up, S duck/climb down, SPACE attack.
Chain hits quickly for COMBOS. Watch telegraphed enemy attacks — dodge, then strike back.
