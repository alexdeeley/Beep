How to Play ✕

Desktop: move the mouse to steer your snake's head — it always turns toward your cursor. Hold Space or click-hold to boost.

Mobile: touch and drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to steer. Tap and hold the glowing button bottom-right to boost.

Eat glowing pellets to grow longer. Boosting burns a small amount of length for a burst of speed.

If your head touches another snake's body, you explode into pellets. Avoid other heads and bodies — but a smaller snake's head can still cut off a bigger one!

Grow the longest snake on the server and climb the leaderboard.
