      Settings

      How to play

      A Braille character appears. Tap the answer that matches it. You get an answer straight away, then the next one comes along by itself.

      How a cell works

      A cell has six dot positions: 1, 2, 3 down the left and 4, 5, 6 down the right. A big solid dot is raised. A small hollow ring is an empty position.

      Numbers

      Braille has no separate cells for digits. A number sign (dots 3-4-5-6) ⠼ comes first, and the cells that follow are read as numbers: A is 1, B is 2 … I is 9 and J is 0. So ⠼⠁ is 1 and ⠼⠁⠃ is 12.

      Modes

        Keyboard

        • 1, 2, 3 choose the first, second or third answer.
        • A letter or digit key chooses that answer when it is on screen.
        • Arrow keys move between answers, Enter or Space picks one.
        • P pauses. Escape goes back.

        Screen readers

        Each cell is described by its raised dots, never by its answer. Results are read out as they happen.
