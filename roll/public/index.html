    How to Play

    On your turn, roll 5 dice up to 3 times. Tap a die to hold it between rolls - held dice don't change on the next roll.

    After any roll, pick one scorecard category to bank it in. Once used, a category is locked for the rest of the game.

    Ones - Sixes: sum of matching dice.
    Three/Four of a Kind: sum of all dice, if at least 3 or 4 match.
    Full House: a pair + a triple - 25 points.
    Small Straight: 4 in a row - 30 points.
    Large Straight: 5 in a row - 40 points.
    Yahtzee: all 5 the same - 50 points.
    Chance: sum of all dice, any combination.

    Fill all 13 categories - score 63+ in the upper section (Ones-Sixes) for a 35-point bonus. Highest total wins!
