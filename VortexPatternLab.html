14. ACCESSIBILITY

Drag on canvas: reposition vortex. Pinch: zoom. Two-finger rotate: spin. Double-tap: randomize. Long-press: freeze/unfreeze.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

  • Space - Play / Pause
  • R - Randomize
  • M - Mutate
  • I - Invert
  • F - Fullscreen
  • H - Hide / Show controls
  • S - Save preset
  • E - Open Export panel
  • 1-9 - Load preset slot
  • Arrow keys - Move pattern center
  • + / - - Zoom
  • [ / ] - Change animation speed
  • Esc - Close panels / exit fullscreen
