Kick, snare, hats, clap, and tom one-shots in this folder are derived from
the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL)
by Versilian Studios LLC, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain -
no attribution required, no royalties, free for any use including
commercial software).

Original files used (from VCSL's `Membranophones` and `Idiophones` folders):

- `kick.wav` <- `Struck Membranophones/Bass Drum 1/BDrumNew_hit_v7_rr1_Sum.wav`
- `snare.wav` <- `Struck Membranophones/Snare Drum, Modern 1/Snare2_HitSN_v9_rr1_Mid.wav`
- `hat-closed.wav` <- `Struck Idiophones/Hi-Hat Cymbal/HiHat_HitC_v4_rr1_Mid.wav`
- `hat-open.wav` <- `Struck Idiophones/Hi-Hat Cymbal/HiHat_HitO_rr1_Mid.wav`
- `clap.wav` <- `Struck Idiophones/Claps/SoloClap_vl4.wav`
- `tom.wav` <- `Struck Membranophones/Tom 1/Stick/TomH_HitS_v4_rr1_Mid.wav`

Each was downmixed to mono, trimmed to the transient plus its natural decay,
peak-normalized, and re-encoded as 16-bit PCM WAV for a smaller, consistent
one-shot set.
